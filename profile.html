About VoxelMD
Project info & feedback
Beta 1.0
Early access — features may change

VoxelMD is an English speaking practice platform built for learners who want to improve pronunciation and fluency through real-world reading material. Paste any Markdown article, listen to AI-generated narration via Kokoro TTS, record yourself reading aloud, and compare your recording side-by-side with the reference audio. Build a personal vocabulary dictionary from words you encounter as you go.

We're currently in Beta 1.0. Sharing, team workspaces, analytics dashboards, and Pro subscription tiers are on the roadmap for Beta 2.0. Your feedback helps us prioritize what ships next.
